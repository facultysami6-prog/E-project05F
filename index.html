About FlameHouse

Fired by Passion, Served with Pride

Born in the heart of Karachi, FlameHouse was built on one belief — great food deserves real fire. We combine traditional BBQ techniques with bold fast food flavors to create an unforgettable dining experience for every Karachiite.

Every item on our menu is crafted with premium ingredients, slow-smoked meats, and spices passed down through generations. This is where your cravings come to life.
